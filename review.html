1

What makes a sentence?

A sentence is a complete thought. Every sentence has these four things:

  • ✓Capital letterat the very start
  • ✓Subjectwho or what it is about
  • ✓Verbwhat happens
  • ✓End marka . ! or ? at the end

The dog ran. All four. That is a sentence!

The dog. What did the dog do? There is no Verb.

ran into the barn. Who ran? There is no Subject, and no capital letter.

2

Kinds of word

Every word has a job. The job is called its part of speech. In the game, each kind of word has its own colour.

Noun

A person, place or thing.

dog · teacher · barn Test: can the go in front? the dog

Pronoun

Takes a noun's place.

He · She · They · him · her He, She and They start a sentence. him and her come after the verb.

Verb

What happens – or was / were.

ran · found · was Test: can it go in front? it ran

Adjective

Describes a noun: what kind?

big · fluffy · sweet Test: does it fit in the ___ dog? the fluffy dog

Adverb

How it happened.

quickly · slowly · happily Many adverbs end in -ly.

Determiner

Comes before a noun.

The · A · My · the · a It points at the noun: my cat, a cat.

Preposition

Where something is, or where it goes.

into · under · near Test: where can a mouse go? into the box, under the box

End mark

Finishes the sentence.

. · ! · ? See End marks below.
3

Sentences are built from blocks

Words team up into blocks. Each block answers a question, and the blocks always come in the same order: the Subject first, then the Verb.

Subjectwho or what?
Verbwhat happens?
Objectwho or what gets it?
Complementwhat is it like?
Modifierhow? where?

Every sentence needs a Subject and a Verb. The other blocks are extras.

4

Inside a block

A Subject or an Object has a Noun at its heart. A Determiner comes first, and you can add an Adjective in the middle:

Determiner+ Adjective+ Noun = Thefluffycat

Or use one Pronoun for the whole block: She

Order matters. An Adjective goes before its Noun: the fluffy cat, not the cat fluffy.

A Modifier is an Adverb, or a Preposition with a place after it:

quickly or intothebarn
5

Little rules that matter

Capital letters

The starts a sentence. the goes in the middle. I always has a capital.

a or an

a dog · an owl · an old cat. Use an before a vowel sound.

He or him

He found the ball. The ball hit him. He does it; him gets it.

was or were

The dog was happy. They were happy. One → was. More than one → were.

Say it once

The happy dog was happy. Pick a new word: The happy dog was sleepy.

Make sense

A cookie tastes sweet, not big. In Real mode, the words have to fit together.

6

End marks

  • .Periodtells something: The dog ran.
  • !Exclamation pointshows a strong feeling: The dragon flew!
  • ?Question markasks something: Did the dog run?

Bookworms sentences tell you something, so your worm finishes them with a . or a ! – once the sentence is complete.
