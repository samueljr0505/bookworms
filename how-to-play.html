How to Play

You are a worm. You eat words. The words make a sentence.

1

Steer your worm

Your worm never stops moving. Use the arrow keys or W A S D to turn it.

↑ ←↓→
or
W ASD
2

Read the strip at the top

The strip shows the shape of the sentence you are building. It always tells you what kind of word to look for next.

Determiner Noun Verb . !

The glowing one is what you need right now.

3

Drive over that kind of word

Words float around the board. Drive your worm into one to eat it.

fox jumped The fluffy duck
Any word of the right kind works. If the strip asks for a Noun, then fox works, duck works, cat works. There is no one secret answer.
Eat the wrong kind and nothing breaks. The game just says what that word was and what you needed instead.
4

Your worm spells the sentence

Every word you eat is written along your worm, one letter per square, in the colour of that kind of word. Read your worm from its tail to its head.

Thefox

Long words make you long – and harder to steer!

5

Finish with punctuation

A sentence is not finished until you close it. Eat a . or a ! and your sentence goes up on your list.

The fox jumped . !

Three words eaten. Now close it.

6

If you crash, you are fine

Hit a wall or bite your own body and you drop the sentence you were building.

  • The words you ate go back onto the board.
  • Your worm starts again in the middle.
  • You keep your points and your finished sentences.

Nothing is lost. Just start the sentence again.

The five kinds of word

Determiner

Points at a noun, and starts the sentence – so it gets a capital letter.

A · The · My

Adjective

Describes a noun.

big · grey · warm

Noun

A person, place or thing.

fox · teacher · apple

Verb

What happens.

jumped · read · is

Adverb

How it happened.

quickly · quietly

Punctuation

Ends the sentence.

. · !
Let's play

P pauses · R starts a new game
