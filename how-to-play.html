How to Play

You are a worm. You eat words. The words make a sentence.

1

Steer your worm

Your worm never stops moving. Use the arrow keys or W A S D to turn it.

↑ ←↓→
or
W ASD
2

Build it from blocks

Every sentence is built from the same building blocks. The strip at the top shows which ones you are building, and what kind of word to look for next.

Subjectwho or what it is about The brave knight · She
Verbwhat happens found · flew · was
Objectwho or what gets the action the map · him
Modifierhow, or where quickly · into the cave

Subject and Verb are a must. The others are extras – add as many as fit: The knight found the map. · She quickly gave him a golden sword. · The dragon was in the tower.

3

Drive over that kind of word

Words float around the board. Drive your worm into one to eat it.

fox jumped The fluffy duck
There is no one secret answer. If the strip asks for a Noun, then fox works, duck works, cat works. But a word also has to make sense where you put it: a cookie can taste sweet, but it cannot taste big.
Every word you eat goes onto your worm – even one that does not fit the strip. You find out how you did when you finish the sentence.
4

Your worm spells the sentence

Every word you eat is written along your worm, one letter per square, in the colour of that kind of word. Read your worm from its tail to its head.

Thefox

Long words make you long – and harder to steer!

5

Finish with punctuation – then it gets checked

A sentence is not finished until you close it. Eat a . or a ! and the checker reads your sentence, the way your teacher would.

The fox jumped . !

Three words eaten. Now close it.

  • ✓Capital letterat the start
  • ✓Nounwho or what it is about
  • ✓Verbwhat happens
  • ✓Sounds rightthe words are in an order that works
  • ✓Makes sensethe words fit together
  • ✓End marka . or a !

All six? Your sentence goes up on your list.

Not finished yet? The checker tells you what is missing – “What happens? Add a Verb.” Your words stay on your worm, so keep building.
Not quite right? The checker tells you which word and why. The words go back on the board so you can try again.
6

If you crash, you are fine

Hit a wall or bite your own body and you drop the sentence you were building.

  • The words you ate go back onto the board.
  • Your worm starts again in the middle.
  • You keep your points and your finished sentences.

Nothing is lost. Just start the sentence again.

⭐

Spend your points in the shop

Every correct sentence earns points. They are saved up, so you can spend them in the Worm Shop on hats, glasses, colours, patterns and sparkly trails.

Get them in a row! Each correct sentence in a row earns a bonus. A wrong sentence never takes away the points you saved.

The kinds of word

Determiner

Comes before a noun. At the start of a sentence it has a capital letter; in the middle it does not.

The · A · My · the · a

Adjective

Describes a noun.

big · grey · warm

Noun

A person, place or thing.

fox · teacher · apple

Verb

What happens.

jumped · read · is

Pronoun

Takes a noun's place. "He" starts a sentence; "him" comes after the verb.

He · She · They · him · her

Preposition

Says where, with a place after it.

into · under · near

Adverb

How it happened.

quickly · quietly

Punctuation

Ends the sentence – and gets it checked.

. · !
