How to Play

You are a worm. You eat words. The words make a sentence.

1

Steer your worm

Your worm never stops moving. Use the arrow keys or W A S D to turn it.

↑ ←↓→
or
W ASD
2

Build it from blocks

Every sentence is built from the same building blocks. The strip at the top shows the blocks in order, and the kind of word that goes next.

Subjectwho or what it is about The brave knight · She
Verbwhat happens found · flew · was
Objectwho or what gets the action the map · him
Complementwhat it is like, after was was wise · was a wizard
Modifierhow, or where quickly · into the cave

Subject and Verb are a must. The others are extras – add as many as fit: The knight found the map. · She quickly gave him a golden sword. · The dragon was in the tower.

Need a reminder of what each block is? Read the Grammar Review.

3

Drive over that kind of word

Words float around the board. Drive your worm into one to eat it.

fox jumped The fluffy duck
There is no one secret answer. If the strip asks for a Noun, then fox works, duck works, cat works. But a word also has to make sense where you put it: a cookie can taste sweet, but it cannot taste big.
Your worm only eats a word that fits next. Bump into one that does not and it stays on the board, and the strip tells you why: “ran is a Verb. After The, the sentence needs a Noun or an Adjective.”
4

Your worm spells the sentence

Every word you eat is written along your worm, one letter per square, in the colour of that kind of word. Whichever way your worm goes, the words read the normal way: left to right, or top to bottom.

Thefox

Long words make you long – and harder to steer!

5

Finish with punctuation

A sentence is not finished until you close it. Once it has every block it needs, eat a . or a ! and it is complete. The game reads it back to you, the way your teacher would.

SubjectThefox
Verbjumped
End. !

A Subject and a Verb. Now close it.

  • ✓Capital letterat the start

  • ✓Verbwhat happens
  • ✓Sounds rightthe words are in an order that works
  • ✓Makes sensethe words fit together
  • ✓End marka . or a !

Your sentence goes up on your list.

Not finished yet? Your worm will not eat the punctuation mark, and the strip tells you what is missing – “What happens? Add a Verb.” Keep building.
6

If you crash, you are fine

Hit a wall or bite your own body and you drop the sentence you were building.

  • The words you ate go back onto the board.
  • Your worm starts again in the middle.
  • You keep your points and your finished sentences.

Nothing is lost. Just start the sentence again.

⭐

Spend your points in the shop

Every sentence earns points. They are saved up, so you can spend them in the Worm Shop on hats, glasses, colours, patterns and sparkly trails.

Get them in a row! Each sentence you build without bumping into a word that does not fit earns a streak bonus. Bumping never takes away the points you saved.
