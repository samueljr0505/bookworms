          How to play
          • Steer with the arrow keys or W A S D.
          • A sentence is built from blocks: a Subject and a Verb, then an Object, a Complement or Modifiers. The strip shows the blocks, and the kind of word that goes next.
          • Your worm only eats a word that fits next. Bump into one that does not and it stays on the board - the strip says why.
          • The starts a sentence; the goes in the middle.
          • Every word you eat is written along your body, and it always reads the normal way: left to right, or top to bottom.
          • When the sentence is whole, eat a punctuation mark (. or !) to finish it.
          • Hit a wall or your own body and the words you were carrying drop back onto the board - go collect them again.
          • Every sentence earns points to spend in the shop on hats, glasses and more. Build several in a row without a bump for a streak bonus!
          📖 Grammar review: Nouns, Verbs and the blocks of a sentence
