      How to play
      • Steer with the arrow keys or W A S D.
      • A sentence is built from blocks: a Subject and a Verb, then an Object or Modifiers if you like. The strip shows the blocks and the kind of word to find next.
      • The starts a sentence; the goes in the middle.
      • Every word you eat is written along your body, and it always reads the normal way: left to right, or top to bottom.
      • Eat a punctuation mark (. or !) to end the sentence. That is when it gets checked: capital letter, noun, verb, sounds right, makes sense, end mark.
      • Not quite right? The checker says why, and the words go back on the board.
      • Hit a wall or your own body and the words you were carrying drop back onto the board - go collect them again.
      • Every correct sentence earns points to spend in the shop on hats, glasses and more. Get several right in a row for a streak bonus!
