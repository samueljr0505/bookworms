Word party

Students write words for your prompt, you approve them, and the class's words become the board. Everyone builds an answer to a new prompt in the snake game, then the class votes for the best one.

  1. Write Students fill in a sentence frame: The [Adjective] [Noun] [Verb] the [Noun] [Adverb].
  2. Approve You say yes or send each answer back, and can strike out any one word.
  3. Build The approved words are chopped up. Students build sentences from them on the board.
  4. Vote Everyone's best sentence goes up without names, and the class votes.

Topic

    The board uses the topic's little words (the, into, him), and tops up from its own words when the class sends too few of a kind.
