      How a sentence gets built

      The game gives you a structure to follow. You do not have to guess what counts as a sentence - the strip above the board always shows the shape you are building, one kind of word at a time.

      1. 1
        Read the structure. Say it asks for this: DeterminerNounVerbAdverb.
      2. 2
        Eat the words in that order. Only a word of the right kind will stick. Eat a wrong one and the game tells you what it was and what you needed. theduckVerbAdverb.
      3. 3
        Finish with punctuation. A sentence is not a sentence until it is closed. Eat a . ! or ? and it goes up on your list. theduckswamslowly.

      The kinds of word

      • Determiner points at a noun: a, the, my
      • Adjective describes a noun: big, grey, hungry
      • Noun names a person, place or thing: duck, garden
      • Verb is what happens: ran, swam, is
      • Adverb tells how or where: slowly, outside
      • Preposition shows where: in, under, near
      • Punctuation ends the sentence: . ! ?

      Every sentence in the game was written out by a person first, so if you follow the structure you will always end up with a real sentence.
