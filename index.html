    How to play

    • Steer with the arrow keys or W A S D.
    • Only the words that fit next are outlined.
    • Every word you eat is written along your body - read your worm from its tail to its head to see your sentence.
    • Eat a punctuation mark (. or !) to end the sentence.
    • Hit a wall or your own body and the words you were carrying drop back onto the board - go collect them again.
